RESPUESTA:
The best option to travel from Barcelona to Reikiavik while spending less than 400 € is by flying directly with Iceland Express, which includes check-in baggage, transportation to the airport, and a night at a hotel if the layover exceeds 8 hours. You can find more information and book your flight through their official website or other travel search engines.
